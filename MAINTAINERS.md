# Maintainers

## Bootstrap status

No repository maintainers or language-review pairs have been appointed in this
new repository.

This is an explicit launch blocker, not a placeholder assignment. Runnable
capsules must not be merged or advertised until the Valkey TSC records:

- at least three repository maintainers;
- a primary and backup reviewer for each admitted language or specialist area;
- the pilot sponsor and escalation path; and
- the owner and date of the pilot checkpoint.

## Responsibilities

Repository maintainers own admission, policy, shared CI, catalog state,
lifecycle decisions, and topology recommendations.

Language and domain reviewers own review quality within their expertise.

Capsule owners own technical accuracy, dependency maintenance, CI failure
response, compatibility updates, and retirement.

The Valkey TSC retains final authority for governance, repository creation,
repository splits, and archival.
